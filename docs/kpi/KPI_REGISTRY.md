# KPI Registry v0.1.0

_Generated by `scripts/export_kpi_registry.py` from `backend/kpi_engine/registry.py`. Do not edit by hand._

Computation model: every KPI is a **ratio of sums** over validated rows. Rows with missing / invalid inputs are excluded from numerator **and** denominator and counted. A KPI is `null` (never 0) when its denominator is <= 0.

### `marketing.roas`: Return on ad spend (ROAS)

Gross revenue generated per unit of advertising spend. 1.0x is break-even on gross revenue.

- **Formula:** `sum(revenue) / sum(mark_spent)`
- **Unit:** ratio_x (x (revenue per 1 unit of spend))
- **Aggregation:** Ratio of sums over the selected rows. Never the average of row-level or group-level ratios.
- **Time window:** Rows whose date falls inside [date_from, date_to] inclusive. Default: the whole dataset period (see dataset declared_period). Trends: day / week (ISO, Mon-Sun) / month / quarter.
- **Min sample before `low_sample` warning:** 10 valid rows
- **Target:** No numeric target exists in OPS-001, PRC-001 or the source notes. Targets are never invented.
- **Source columns (`marketing_daily`):** `revenue`, `mark_spent`
- **Limitations:**
  - Revenue is gross, not margin: ROAS 1.0 is not profit break-even.
  - Single month (2021-02) of public data: no seasonality, not current.
  - Ranking is sensitive to outlier days (e.g. youtube_blogger spend 880k on one day).
  - Spend-weighted: a simple mean of campaign ROAS (1.370) differs from total ROAS (1.402).

- **Time window:** Rows whose date falls inside [date_from, date_to] inclusive. Default: the whole dataset period (see dataset declared_period). Trends: day / week (ISO, Mon-Sun) / month / quarter.

  - Single month (2021-02) of public data.

- **Aggregation:** Ratio of sums over the selected rows. Never the average of row-level or group-level ratios.
- **Time window:** Rows whose date falls inside [date_from, date_to] inclusive. Default: the whole dataset period (see dataset declared_period). Trends: day / week (ISO, Mon-Sun) / month / quarter.
- **Min sample before `low_sample` warning:** 10 valid rows
- **Target:** No numeric target exists in OPS-001, PRC-001 or the source notes. Targets are never invented.
- **Source columns (`marketing_daily`):** `mark_spent`, `orders`
- **Limitations:**
  - Currency is not stated in the source.
  - Undefined (value None) when total orders in the selection is 0; never reported as 0 or infinity.
  - 19 campaign-days have zero orders; their spend stays in the numerator (it is real cost).
  - Single month (2021-02) of public data.

### `procurement.savings_pct`: Procurement savings

Negotiated discount versus list price, weighted by PO value.

- **Formula:** `sum(Quantity * (Unit_Price - Negotiated_Price)) / sum(Quantity * Unit_Price) * 100`
- **Unit:** percent (% of list value)
- **Aggregation:** Ratio of sums over the selected rows. Never the average of row-level or group-level ratios.
- **Time window:** Rows whose date falls inside [date_from, date_to] inclusive. Default: the whole dataset period (see dataset declared_period). Trends: day / week (ISO, Mon-Sun) / month / quarter.
- **Min sample before `low_sample` warning:** 10 valid rows
- **Target:** No numeric target exists in OPS-001, PRC-001 or the source notes. Targets are never invented.
- **Source columns (`procurement_po`):** `Quantity`, `Unit_Price`, `Negotiated_Price`
- **Limitations:**
  - Unit_Price is treated as the list/baseline price; the source does not define it.
  - Cancelled and Pending POs are included by default (this reproduces the published supplier/quarter files); filter status to restrict to realised orders. Savings on cancelled POs are not realised.
  - Nearly flat across suppliers (7.8% to 8.3%): fine as a headline, weak for ranking.
  - Prices are similar across categories, so spend mixes unit types.
  - Currency is not stated in the source.

### `procurement.avg_lead_time_days`: Average lead time

Mean days between order date and delivery date over POs with a valid delivery date.

- **Formula:** `mean(Delivery_Date - Order_Date) over rows with both dates and Delivery_Date >= Order_Date`
- **Unit:** days (days)
- **Aggregation:** Arithmetic mean over valid records in the selected rows (sum / count). Records are not weighted.
- **Time window:** Rows whose date falls inside [date_from, date_to] inclusive. Default: the whole dataset period (see dataset declared_period). Trends: day / week (ISO, Mon-Sun) / month / quarter.
- **Min sample before `low_sample` warning:** 10 valid rows
- **Target:** No numeric target exists in OPS-001, PRC-001 or the source notes. Targets are never invented.
- **Source columns (`procurement_po`):** `Order_Date`, `Delivery_Date`
- **Limitations:**
  - 87 POs have no delivery date (68 of them marked Delivered): likely a data gap, excluded not imputed.
  - 1 PO (PO-00101) has delivery before order (-5 days): excluded.
  - Cancelled/Pending POs also carry delivery dates; the default keeps them (reproduces the published files). A warning states how many are included; filter status=Delivered for a stricter view.
  - Orders are placed by Order_Date: the last quarter (2024Q1) holds only 2 POs.

- **Unit:** percent (% of units)
- **Aggregation:** Ratio of sums over the selected rows. Never the average of row-level or group-level ratios.
- **Time window:** Rows whose date falls inside [date_from, date_to] inclusive. Default: the whole dataset period (see dataset declared_period). Trends: day / week (ISO, Mon-Sun) / month / quarter.
- **Min sample before `low_sample` warning:** 10 valid rows
- **Target:** No numeric target exists in OPS-001, PRC-001 or the source notes. Targets are never invented.
- **Source columns (`procurement_po`):** `Defective_Units`, `Quantity`
- **Limitations:**

  - Resolves the open R03 formula question in kpi_column_map.csv: this definition reproduces the published supplier and quarter summary files exactly.
